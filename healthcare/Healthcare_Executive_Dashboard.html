Healthcare Network Analytics – Executive Dashboard

Patient demand, billing and emergency activity across the network: what leadership needs to know.

Reporting period: May 2019 – May 2024

Executive Summary

Key Business Insights

Arthritis is the most common condition, but demand is evenly spread

Arthritis (9,218) leads; Asthma is lowest (9,095), a gap of only 1.3%. Click a bar to filter.

Patients are mid-life on average, from teens to late 80s

Average age is 51.5; the middle half of patients are aged 35–68.

Average billing moves month to month without a sustained climb

Monthly average billing swings roughly between 24.5K and 26.8K around the overall 25.6K average; May 2024 (25,465) ended 1.7% below May 2019 (25,897).

Monthly values other than the first and last five months are read from the analysis chart and are approximate.

Age does not drive cost

Patients aged 60+ average 25,564 per admission versus 25,595 overall, a difference of just 31 (0.1%).

Cigna covers the most patients, yet insurers are almost equal

Cigna (11,139) leads, Aetna is lowest (10,822); every provider holds 19.7%–20.3%. Click a slice to filter.

Emergency admissions are steady all year, with peaks in July and January

18,102 emergency admissions across the period; monthly totals range from 1,452 (February) to 1,573 (July), grouped by calendar month.
